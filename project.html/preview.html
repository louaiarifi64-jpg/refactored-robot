✧ Engineer ✧

What makes this job special:

Engineers design buildings, machines and technology.

Why people choose it:

People choose engineering because they like inventions and problem solving.

Role in society:

Engineers build roads, houses and bridges.

Important achievements:

Modern buildings, bridges, and machines were created by engineers.

✧ Doctor ✧

What makes this job special:

Doctors treat sick people and save lives.

Why people choose it:

People choose this job because they want to help others.

Role in society:

Doctors keep people healthy.

Important achievements:

Vaccines and modern medicine save millions of lives.

✧ Teacher ✧

What makes this job special:

Teachers educate students and share knowledge.

Why people choose it:

People choose teaching because they like education.

Role in society:

Teachers build the future of society.

Important achievements:

Schools and education systems help develop societies.

✧ Firefighter ✧

What makes this job special:

Firefighters stop fires and rescue people.

Why people choose it:

People choose this job because they are brave.

Role in society:

Firefighters protect homes and people.

Important achievements:

Modern fire trucks and rescue equipment help firefighters.

✧ Police Officer ✧

What makes this job special:

Police officers protect people and enforce the law.

Why people choose it:

People choose this job to protect society.

Role in society:

Police officers keep the community safe.

Important achievements:

Modern investigation tools help police officers.
