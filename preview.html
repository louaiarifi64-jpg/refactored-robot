Engineer

Engineers design buildings and machines. They build bridges and roads. Many people choose this job because they like technology. Engineers help to build modern cities. This job needs intelligence and work. Engineers make life easier. It is an important profession.

Doctor

Doctors help sick people. They work in hospitals. Many people choose this job to save lives. Doctors use medicines. They protect health. Doctors help society. It is a noble job.

Teacher

Teachers educate students. They teach knowledge. Many people choose this job because they love teaching. Teachers build the future. Education is important. Teachers prepare new generations. It is a respected job.

Firefighter

Firefighters fight fires. They save people. Many people choose this job to help others. They protect houses. Firefighters are brave. They save lives. It is a heroic job.

Police Officer

Police officers protect people. They fight crime. Many people choose this job for justice. Police keep safety. They work day and night. Police help society. It is an important job.

Sweet Maker

Sweet makers prepare cakes and sweets. They work in bakeries. Many people choose this job because they love cooking. Sweet makers make people happy. They prepare delicious food. This job needs patience. It is a useful job.

Barber

Barbers cut hair. They work in barber shops. Many people choose this job because they like working with people. Barbers help people look nice. They use scissors and machines. This job needs skill. It is an important job.

Farmer

Farmers grow fruits and vegetables. They work in farms. Many people choose this job because they love nature. Farmers produce food. They help society. This job needs hard work. It is essential.

Builder

Builders construct houses. They work in construction sites. Many people choose this job because they like building. Builders use tools. They build cities. This job needs strength. It is a useful job.
